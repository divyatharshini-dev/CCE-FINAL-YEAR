Types of Network Topology

Most commonly used topologies in college & office networks

1. Star Topology

[Switch] / | \ [PC1] [PC2] [PC3]

All devices connect to a central Switch. If one PC fails, others still work. Most used in colleges.

Most Reliable | Used in Our Lab

2. Bus Topology

[PC1] - [PC2] - [PC3] Single Main Cable

All devices share one main cable. Cheap but if main cable breaks, full network stops.
